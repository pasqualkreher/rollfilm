Scene cubes for more analog film looks (F-Log2 / F-Gamut code values in,
display sRGB out), baked by backend/tools/film_sim_fit/bake_spectral.py.

Derived from spectral_film_lut by Jan Lohse
https://github.com/JanLohse/spectral_film_lut (commit b8d69398fc71038579c20473378be22103695dd5)
Licensed MIT - copyright and permission notice in SPECTRAL_FILM_LUT_LICENSE.txt,
next to this file.
Modified by Pasqual Kreher: the stocks are rendered into 3D LUTs for Rollfilm,
each negative on the print named below, and levelled - the medium's black and
white on the display's, 18% grey on middle grey.

kodak_vericolor_iii.npy
    Kodak Vericolor III printed on Kodak Portra Endura Paper
kodak_aerocolor_iv.npy
    Kodak Aerocolor IV 2460 printed on Kodak Portra Endura Paper
fuji_pro_160s.npy
    Fuji Pro 160S printed on Fuji Crystal Archive DPII
fuji_natura_1600.npy
    Fuji Natura 1600 printed on Fuji Crystal Archive DPII
fuji_eterna_500.npy
    Fuji Eterna 500 printed on Fuji Eterna-CP Type 3513DI
fuji_eterna_500_vivid.npy
    Fuji Eterna 500 Vivid printed on Fuji Eterna-CP Type 3513DI
fuji_instax_color.npy
    Fuji Instax color as its own positive

The trademarked names are there to say which film stock a cube is made to
approximate. Neither the author nor Rollfilm are affiliated with or endorsed
by the companies that own them.

Removing a file takes its look out of the scene path.
